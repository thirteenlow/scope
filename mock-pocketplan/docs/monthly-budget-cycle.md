# Monthly budget cycle

1. A user opens a budget month.
2. Income available to budget is calculated from cash-account inflows.
3. The user assigns available money to categories such as Groceries, Transport and Rent.
4. Cleared and pending transactions reduce each category's activity balance.
5. Available amount is calculated as assigned minus activity for that month.
6. On the first day of a new month, PocketPlan creates a fresh budget month.
7. Category assignments start at zero; unused positive balances are not carried forward.
8. Overspent categories are shown in the previous month and do not automatically reduce the next month.

Savings goals are separate targets. They may suggest a monthly contribution, but they do not move money or preserve category balances automatically.
